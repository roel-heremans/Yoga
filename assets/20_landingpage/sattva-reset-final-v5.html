TWO TEACHERS. TWO WINGS.

ONE SPACE.

NINA

Kundalini Yoga & Sound Healing

Born into a family of yoga teachers, Nina was introduced to the path of yoga at an early age through her mother's guidance. At 16, she discovered Kundalini Yoga — the beginning of a lifelong spiritual journey.

At 21, she completed intensive teacher training in London and went on to work closely within the teachings of Yogi Bhajan, further deepening her studies in India. After teaching in Taiwan and Hong Kong, Nina returned to Germany and joined Balance Yoga in Frankfurt, where she met Roel.

With more than 25 years of teaching experience and ongoing training with master teachers from various yogic lineages, Nina has become a respected expert in Kundalini Yoga, sound and crystal healing. Her spiritual exploration has led her to integrate multiple healing modalities into her work, creating a holistic and embodied approach to transformation.

Nina is certified in Kundalini Yoga (since 2001), Hatha Yoga, Prenatal Yoga, and Pranic Healing. Today, she leads 300+ hour Kundalini Yoga teacher trainings in partnership with a yoga studio in Germany.

A Kundalini Yoga teacher and Light Priestess, Nina now lives on Madeira Island, where she shares her passion for yoga, wellness, and spirituality. She is also a wellness advocate for essential oils, supporting people in discovering natural solutions for their health.

ROEL

Hatha Yoga & Pranayama

Roel is a 500-hour RYT Hatha Yoga teacher who began his teaching journey in 2014. Originally trained in Hong Kong and later completing his 500-hour Hatha Yoga teacher training in Tibet. His path into yoga grew from a background in data science and academia into a lifelong dedication to intelligent movement and conscious practice.

His teaching style is dynamic, progressive, and precise. With a strong focus on alignment and intelligent sequencing, Roel guides students to deepen their asana practice safely and sustainably. Inversions are one of his specialties — always introduced with clarity and care — offering students the opportunity to shift perspective, both physically and mentally.

Breathwork and yogic philosophy are woven seamlessly into his structured and thoughtfully designed classes. For Roel, every asana has a purpose. Every breath counts.

Holding a PhD in High Energy Particle Physics, he remains deeply curious about the intersection of yoga, science, and metaphysics — exploring how ancient wisdom and modern understanding inform and enrich one another.

He has shared his teaching in diverse environments, including corporate and research institutions such as the European Space Agency, bridging structured analytical thinking with embodied awareness.
