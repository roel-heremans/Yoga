# PDF Sources - Quick Reference

This document provides direct links and search terms to find relevant PDFs for each theme.

## General Research Sources

### Free Academic Databases:
1. **PubMed** - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/
   - Free access to medical research
   - Many papers have free PDF links

2. **ResearchGate** - https://www.researchgate.net/
   - Researchers share their papers
   - Many free PDFs available

3. **Google Scholar** - https://scholar.google.com/
   - Search academic papers
   - Look for "PDF" links on the right side

4. **PMC (PubMed Central)** - https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/
   - Free full-text articles
   - Many kombucha-related papers available

## How to Download PDFs

### From PubMed:
1. Search for papers
2. Click on paper title
3. Look for "Free full text" or "PDF" link
4. Download and save to appropriate theme folder

### From ResearchGate:
1. Search for papers
2. Click "Read" or "Download" button
3. May require free account signup
4. Download and save to appropriate theme folder

### From Google Scholar:
1. Search for papers
2. Look for "PDF" link on the right
3. Click to download
4. Save to appropriate theme folder

### From Websites:
1. Find article online
2. Use browser "Print" function
3. Choose "Save as PDF"
4. Save to appropriate theme folder

## Tips

1. **Start with review articles** - they summarize multiple studies
2. **Look for open access papers** - free to download
3. **Save PDFs with descriptive names** - e.g., "kombucha-probiotics-2020.pdf"
4. **Focus on recent papers** - last 5-10 years when possible
5. **Mix scientific and accessible** - both research papers and health articles work
